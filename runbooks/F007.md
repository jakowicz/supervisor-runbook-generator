## Objective

Create `projects/<project-slug>/planning/07-feature-experience-contracts.md`
with atomic implementation-contract records for every first-release feature,
journey, responsive experience, accessibility requirement, and error/recovery
flow in F005.

For games, turn every selected module's player-facing rules and production
inventory into contracts. Include the core loop, content consumption, feedback,
progression, failure/recovery, and relevant content authoring/validation flow.
An RPG may produce contracts for exploration, battle, quests, party progression
and content presentation; football trivia may produce question selection,
answer input and adjudication, scoring/timers, repeat avoidance, game modes,
and accessible feedback. Do not substitute a generic “gameplay” contract for
these genre-specific outcomes.

## Acceptance criteria

- Records define user-visible behaviour, invariants, permissions, validation,
  and persistence/recovery semantics.
- Browser-impacting records own specific UI journeys and test evidence.
- Original design, accessibility, and scope boundaries are testable requirements
  rather than broad aspirations.
