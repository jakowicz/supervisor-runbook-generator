## Objective

Create `projects/<project-slug>/specification/03-technical-contract.md` from F001–F002. Define
stack boundaries, domain entities, validation, authorization, persistence,
migrations, idempotency, concurrency, failure semantics, privacy, security,
observability, and release verification. Include a target-platform matrix for
the systems selected in `INITIAL.md`, covering shared code/data boundaries,
input, device capabilities, offline behaviour, builds, release channels, and
platform-policy constraints.

Choose the technical and production architecture demanded by the discovered
domain. This may include deterministic simulation and data-driven content
systems; workflow, collaboration, sync, scheduling, and integrations; language
tooling and extension hosts; low-level system services; or authoring/import/
export/editor tools. Do not force any one architecture style onto a product.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each technical component traces to a product or quality requirement.
- Data ownership, lifecycle, authorization, recovery, and test evidence are explicit.
- External services remain optional until credentials, cost, and failure behaviour are approved.
- Each selected target has a build, test, accessibility, performance, and release strategy.
- High-volume content has a versioned schema, ownership, validation, import/
  export or authoring path, and testable relationship rules before content tasks
  are generated.
