# Product brief

## Requested outcome

Describe the application in one or two sentences.

## Users and core jobs

- Who will use it?
- What must they accomplish in the first useful session?

## Required capabilities

- List the must-have outcomes, distinguishing first-release work from later work.

## Constraints and non-goals

- Platforms, technology, privacy, accessibility, offline, integration, cost, and delivery constraints.

## Reference boundaries

List products used only as functional references. Their brands, copy, assets,
screenshots, layouts, and distinctive interaction choreography must not be copied.

## Open decisions

- Record decisions needing product or technical approval.
