## Objective

Create `projects/<project-slug>/planning/runbook-generation-quality-gate.md`
and validation instructions/scripts that can be run after every B-series wave.
Validate the authoring collection itself and the R-series contracts it produces.
The validation is structural and traceability-based; it must not claim the
product has been implemented or released.

## Acceptance criteria

- Validation checks required front matter, unique IDs, sequence ordering,
  requirement links, dependency closure, output ownership, and unsupported
  circular dependencies. Every R contract must also have non-empty provenance:
  canonical `source_specifications`, `source_catalogue_ids`, its originating
  `authoring_batch`, and valid `factory_stages`; the links must resolve to the
  manifest and canonical specification rather than merely naming an F or B
  procedure file.
- Validation checks every R-series contract has an explicit asset assessment:
  `asset_impact` is `required` or `not_applicable`, `asset_ids` is present,
  asset-required contracts reserve non-empty unique stable IDs and an asset
  brief/style version, and non-asset contracts do not silently introduce asset
  work. It reports missing, duplicate, ambiguous, or unowned asset IDs.
- Validation checks every R-series contract has an explicit audio assessment:
  `audio_impact` is `required` or `not_applicable`; audio-required contracts
  reserve non-empty unique stable cue IDs, a brief, a positive duration, loop
  policy, style version, and source link to `04-audio-direction.md`; unrelated
  contracts do not silently introduce music or sound-effect work.
- When `04-asset-direction.md` or `04-audio-direction.md` exists, validation
  checks a coverage map: every required asset family and audio cue-map entry is
  owned by a current or queued `IMP-*` contract, and every allocated such
  contract has `asset_impact: required` and/or `audio_impact: required` with
  stable IDs. It fails if a game/product declares visual or audio direction yet
  the catalogue has no asset/audio-required work outside a documented future
  shard.
- The validator parses the exact family and cue IDs from the canonical
  direction files and compares them against `asset_audio_coverage`; it rejects
  missing IDs, ambiguous ownership, a generic schema/provenance/documentation
  contract used as sole coverage, a queue entry with no named eligible shard,
  or an expanded family/cue whose allocated R contract is not marked required
  with matching stable IDs and direction provenance. It also rejects a final
  audit while any declared family/cue is still only an unexpanded placeholder.
- It detects duplicate browser test paths and confirms browser-impact metadata
  only where browser behaviour is actually in scope.
- It reports catalogue/manifest/specification coverage counts and identifies
  every missing, duplicate, blocked, or unverified contract by ID.
- It fails the quality gate when a discovered domain family has no specification
  chapter, content/authoring approach where applicable, implementation
  contracts, or traceable verification. It checks category-appropriate
  completeness rather than applying a fixed checklist to every product.
- It includes recovery: how to rerun one failed B-series authoring task, repair
  a bounded R-series range, and resume the next dispatcher wave without
  regenerating accepted work.
- It rejects an authoring collection that treats an unresolved implementation
  gate or R-to-R dependency as a reason not to create an allocated R contract.
  A valid R contract records the gate; only later product-work execution waits.
- Human gates, paid services, credentials, legal/compliance decisions, device
  access, and store/certification actions remain explicit review points.
