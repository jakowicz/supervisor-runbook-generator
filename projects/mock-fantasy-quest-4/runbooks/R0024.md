## Objective

Define settings and accessible-feedback contracts for the shared web/PWA UI:
text, contrast, non-colour state cues, motion, master/music/effects controls,
and caption visibility. They make preference state and its honest persistence
outcome available without selecting storage, audio assets, locales, or an
assistive-technology support matrix.

## Dependencies

R0010 supplies the accessibility foundation, R0006 supplies independent local
preference recovery, and R0022 supplies settings navigation and focus routing.
This contract consumes their public states. GATE-0002 retains the approved
browser/device/AT matrix, GATE-0003 retains storage policy, and GATE-0004
retains audio, localisation, font, and contrast production choices.

## Ownership

- `src/app/settings/`, `src/app/accessibility/`, and `tests/app/accessibility/`.
- Local preference and caption view state, plus settings and accessible-status
  API contracts.
- Preference isolation, failure presentation, and reset-confirmation rules.

## Required behaviour

Settings are reachable before a new game and while paused. Expose text size,
higher contrast, non-colour state markers, motion reduction, caption
visibility, and master/music/effects controls with visible labels and current
state. Health, enemy intent, quest/save state, and map interactions retain
text equivalents; settings and status changes have concise, deduplicated
accessible feedback rather than animation-frame announcements.

Caption state controls concise speaker and relevant-sound text for every
non-text audio cue that conveys story, instruction, combat state, or warning.
It reserves no cues, assets, timing, language, or mix. Reduced motion lets a
player pause, slow, or disable nonessential movement and screen effects; no
essential dialogue, choice, error, or recovery message times out.

Preferences are separate from campaign saves. On a preference-write failure,
retain the current session choice, say that it will not persist, and keep a
usable default; do not claim persistence succeeded. Reset identifies affected
preference categories, starts with a safe Cancel action, and changes no
campaign, checkpoint, cache, diagnostic, or recovery data.

## Web and PWA differences

Responsive web and installed PWA share this preference model, caption state,
and semantic feedback. Storage selection, service workers, cache lifecycle,
installation, update activation, and a compatibility promise remain outside
this contract.

## Non-goals

Do not create audio or visual assets, select locales, choose storage, claim
WCAG conformance or a support matrix, implement cloud preferences, alter
campaign saves, or resolve GATE-0002, GATE-0003, or GATE-0004.

## Asset assessment

`asset_impact` is `not_applicable`: settings, captions, and feedback rules
create no product asset. `asset_ids` remains blank; do not reserve fonts,
icons, mockups, illustrations, or caption/audio assets.

## Audio assessment

`audio_impact` is `not_applicable`: this contract exposes preference and
caption state but selects no cue, playback, mix, or source audio. Keep audio
metadata blank/default and defer audio scope to GATE-0004.

## Verification

- Use deterministic fixtures to prove reduced-motion, text/non-colour state,
  caption visibility, and accessible-status changes preserve semantic labels.
- Prove a failed preference write retains the session choice, reports its
  non-persistence, and cannot affect campaign saves; prove reset starts on
  Cancel and is preference-only.
- Compare metadata, requirements, provenance, dependencies, and ownership to
  IMP-0024 and the manifest while retaining all three gates.

## Evidence

Retain preference-isolation, write-failure, reset-confirmation, caption/motion,
and semantic-status fixtures; manifest/catalogue comparison; and the retained
GATE-0002, GATE-0003, and GATE-0004 records.

## Acceptance criteria

- Settings isolate accessible preferences from campaign saves and cover motion,
  captions, text, contrast, non-colour feedback, and honest write failure.
- No audio/visual asset, locale, cloud preference, compatibility, or WCAG
  conformance claim is introduced.
- GATE-0002, GATE-0003, and GATE-0004 remain explicit implementation gates.
