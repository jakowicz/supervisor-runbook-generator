## Objective

Define assembly of one versioned original campaign package that references the
town, dungeon, and character asset groups and connects the SHARD-02 domain
contracts to the existing content and provenance controls. Reserve assets only;
do not create product expression during authoring.

## Dependencies

R0005 owns schema, validation, registry, and package loading. R0011 owns the
provenance record and mandatory human creative-review disposition. R0013–R0016
provide the content-neutral domain contracts referenced by the package. This
contract assembles their allowed references without changing their owners.
GATE-0004 and the human provenance review block implementation/admission of
concrete authored content; neither is resolved here.

## Ownership

- `content/campaign/`, `assets/source-manifest.*`, and
  `tests/content/campaign*`.
- Campaign package assembly, stable content references, and provenance-manifest
  entries for the reserved asset groups.
- Content package loader integration using the R0005 validation boundary.

## Required behaviour

Assemble a versioned package for exactly one town, one dungeon, and the
campaign's character/content references. Every expressive package entry must
have a stable content ID, package reference, and complete provenance record as
defined by R0011. The loader admits only a package whose references resolve,
whose provenance is complete, and whose human creative-review disposition is
recorded as ready; it must reject missing, unmatched, or unreviewed entries.
The package must use the predecessor contracts' stable IDs rather than define
new gameplay rules. A reviewer may hold or reject the package; automated checks
cannot claim creative, legal, licensing, or GATE-0004 approval.

## Web and PWA differences

The same validated package is consumed by web and installed-PWA adapters.
Rendering, responsive presentation, packaging, caching, and download behavior
remain owned by later UI/PWA work and do not bypass provenance validation.

## Non-goals

Do not create, generate, source, select, or approve artwork, maps, characters,
dialogue, localisation, music, sound effects, licences, or copied expression.
Do not add regions, UI, PWA delivery, or legal approval; do not resolve
GATE-0004 or the human review.

## Asset assessment

`asset_impact` is `required`. Reserve only these stable product asset groups:
`ASSET-CAMPAIGN-TOWN` (one original town environment),
`ASSET-CAMPAIGN-DUNGEON` (one original dungeon environment), and
`ASSET-CAMPAIGN-CHARACTERS` (original campaign character expression). They
appear only through package references and provenance entries; their source of
truth is the later approved asset/content implementation. Required variants,
visual style, and original-art acceptance remain unselected under
`unselected-gate-0004`. Authoring creates no files or product assets. Before
admission, each eventual asset must have complete provenance and an explicit
human creative-review disposition; technical validation is not approval.

## Audio assessment

`audio_impact` is `not_applicable`: no campaign cue is selected or integrated
by this assembly contract. Keep audio IDs, brief, duration, loop policy, and
style version blank/default; do not create incidental audio.

## Verification

- Validate a synthetic, non-product package whose town/dungeon/character
  references resolve through R0005 and whose domain references are stable.
- Prove absent provenance, unmatched references, invalid disposition, or a
  package not marked ready after human review is rejected before admission.
- Check that the three reserved IDs, asset brief, and style placeholder match
  IMP-0018 and the manifest, with no asset created; retain GATE-0004 and the
  recorded human provenance-review requirement.

## Evidence

Retain package-validator results, rejected provenance fixtures, the synthetic
human-review disposition, asset-reservation comparison, and both gate records.

## Acceptance criteria

- The versioned package resolves only validated, stable campaign references.
- Every expressive entry requires complete provenance and a recorded human
  creative-review disposition before admission.
- Only the three manifest-defined asset IDs are reserved; no product asset,
  copied expression, or creative/legal approval is created or claimed.
