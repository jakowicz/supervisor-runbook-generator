## Objective

Add deterministic shared-core negative fixtures and cross-domain invariants for
the SHARD-02 campaign contracts. The work proves that invalid references,
blocked routes, stale commands, and duplicate grants are detected without
introducing concrete campaign content.

## Dependencies

R0013 supplies scene/exit validation, R0014 quest transitions, R0015 encounter
and reward resolution, and R0016 inventory/party protection. This contract owns
fixtures and invariant assertions only; it does not change their APIs or state
rules. GATE-0004 remains explicit for concrete content assumptions.

## Ownership

- `tests/core/campaign/fixtures/` and `tests/core/campaign/invariants*`.
- Deterministic negative campaign fixtures and fixture builders.
- Cross-contract invariant assertions and repeat-safety coverage.

## Required behaviour

Provide deterministic fixture builders for unresolved scene, quest, encounter,
reward, item, predicate, and route references; blocked and dead-end routes;
stale revisions; and duplicate interaction/reward IDs. Each fixture has a
stable expected rejection or invariant result. Assert that invalid inputs never
mutate campaign state, required completion routes remain reachable in valid
abstract fixtures, and retries do not duplicate rewards or progression. Keep
fixtures content-neutral and isolated from player data.

## Web and PWA differences

These are shared-core test fixtures. They do not test browser rendering, input,
responsive UI, service workers, cache behavior, or installed-PWA lifecycle.

## Non-goals

Do not create product maps, dialogue, characters, combatants, assets, audio,
real player data, browser-matrix tests, UI, persistence drivers, or concrete
content values. Do not resolve GATE-0004.

## Asset assessment

`asset_impact` is `not_applicable`: deterministic invalid-state fixtures require
no product visual/source asset. Do not create or reserve maps, sprites, icons,
textures, portraits, fonts, or other incidental assets; `asset_ids` is blank.

## Audio assessment

`audio_impact` is `not_applicable`: fixtures own no music or sound-effect
trigger. Keep audio metadata blank/default and do not create test or product
cues.

## Verification

- Run every fixture twice and prove byte-for-byte equivalent result, including
  unchanged state for invalid reference, blocked route, and stale-command cases.
- Prove duplicate interaction/reward IDs never apply twice and a valid abstract
  fixture has a reachable completion path with no protected-item dead end.
- Compare requirements, dependencies, ownership, and provenance against
  IMP-0019 and the manifest; retain GATE-0004 for concrete assumptions.

## Evidence

Retain deterministic fixture output, invariant failures/rejections, repeat-run
comparison, reachability output, and manifest/catalogue comparison.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every invalid campaign reference and blocked route has deterministic detected
  coverage and does not mutate state.
- Valid abstract progression has no dead end; stale commands and duplicate
  grants are repeat-safe.
- Fixtures introduce no player data, browser coverage, product assets, audio,
  or concrete content, while GATE-0004 remains explicit.
