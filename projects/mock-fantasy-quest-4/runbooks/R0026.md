## Objective

Define deterministic SHARD-03 integration evidence that cross-checks session,
semantic shell, input adapters, accessible settings, and recoverable UI states.
It produces a traceable evidence matrix and handoff to SHARD-04 without a
real-device certification, browser/device matrix, PWA lifecycle, release
approval, or product asset.

## Dependencies

R0021–R0025 own the session, shell, input, settings, and recovery contracts.
This runbook only coordinates their public fixtures and must not change their
APIs or duplicate their ownership. GATE-0002 retains compatibility and numeric
release evidence; GATE-0003 retains storage policy; GATE-0004 retains content,
audio, localisation, and visual-production choices. SHARD-04 remains queued
until its separate GATE-0002, GATE-0005, and GATE-0007 handoff conditions.

## Ownership

- `tests/app/accessibility-integration/` and `docs/ui-handoff.md`.
- Accessibility/input evidence matrix and documented SHARD-04 handoff.
- Cross-contract fixture composition and provenance review only.

## Required behaviour

Create deterministic fixtures that cover each SHARD-03 contract exactly once:
R0021 session/checkpoint honesty; R0022 semantic landmarks, panel/focus/reflow;
R0023 keyboard, pointer, touch, gamepad equivalence and repeat safety; R0024
preferences, captions, motion, non-colour cues, and independent failure; and
R0025 recovery, destructive confirmation, and diagnostics minimisation.

The matrix records requirement IDs, source/catalougue provenance, owner,
fixture, expected invariant, evidence location, and retained gate. It checks
visible focus, accurate accessible names, semantic assistive-input operation,
keyboard/touch/mouse/gamepad alternatives, captions, reduced motion, 44 CSS-px
touch-target review, 200% text/reflow review, clear next action, and safe
save/recovery/update boundaries as contract criteria—not as a device or release
certification.

Handoff to SHARD-04 names the evidence it may consume: deterministic
cross-contract fixture traces, gate records, and explicitly unverified
browser/device/performance/release evidence. It must not implement or imply a
service worker, cached-release policy, real-device test result, compatibility
matrix, hosting, certification, or release approval.

## Web and PWA differences

The evidence compares shared web/PWA semantic contracts only. Browser/device
coverage, PWA installation, service-worker/cache/update lifecycle,
performance budgets, hosting, and release evidence belong to queued SHARD-04
after its gates; no browser spec is introduced by this documentation contract.

## Non-goals

Do not certify devices, implement product UI/PWA behaviour, create assets or
audio, change prior owners, resolve gates, or advance/allocate SHARD-04.

## Asset assessment

`asset_impact` is `not_applicable`: the matrix and handoff create no product

## Audio assessment
