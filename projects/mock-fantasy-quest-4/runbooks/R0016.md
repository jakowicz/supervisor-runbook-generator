## Objective

Define eligibility-checked inventory, party progression, and stable reward
application rules that protect required items and prevent dead ends. This task
owns shared contracts, not authored items, values, presentation, or economy.

## Dependencies

R0004 provides immutable state and idempotent commands; R0015 provides stable
reward resolution and one-time reward IDs. Their implementation ownership
remains intact. GATE-0004 remains an implementation gate for authored item and
progression shapes; it does not defer this contract's authoring.

## Ownership

- `src/core/campaign/{inventory,party}*` and
  `tests/core/campaign/inventory*`.
- Item, eligibility, party-progression, and reward-application envelopes.
- Shared inventory commands and protected-required-item invariants.

## Required behaviour

Model inventory and party progression using stable identifiers, explicit
eligibility predicates, and immutable transitions. Validate item existence,
ownership, expected revision, target eligibility, prerequisite state, and the
originating reward before mutation. Ineligible use, missing items, duplicate
reward application, and stale commands are no-ops with typed rejection or the
recorded prior outcome. Required items cannot be destructively discarded,
consumed, traded, or overwritten in a way that makes the required abstract
route impossible.

## Web and PWA differences

Web and PWA adapters submit the same inventory/progression commands and render
the returned state/events. Inventory controls, drag/drop, responsive layout,
notifications, persistence-driver choices, and installed-PWA lifecycle are
outside this contract.

## Non-goals

Do not author items, party members, progression values, inventory UI, crafting,
trading, purchases, destructive discard, art, audio, maps, remote state, or
browser/PWA behavior. Do not resolve GATE-0004.

## Asset assessment

`asset_impact` is `not_applicable`: abstract inventory and party rules need no
visual/source asset. Do not create or reserve item icons, portraits, sprites,
textures, fonts, illustrations, maps, or other product assets; `asset_ids`
remains blank.

## Audio assessment

`audio_impact` is `not_applicable`: this contract owns no music or sound-effect
trigger, selection, or mix. Keep audio IDs, brief, and style version blank and
do not create incidental cues.

## Verification

- Use deterministic fixtures to prove eligibility is checked before mutation
  and that missing/ineligible/stale commands preserve the exact prior state.
- Prove duplicate reward IDs do not apply twice and protected required items
  cannot be lost in a way that removes the required abstract route.
- Compare metadata, requirements, dependencies, ownership, and provenance with
  IMP-0016 and R0016's manifest allocation; retain GATE-0004 explicitly.

## Evidence

Retain inventory/progression rejection fixtures, duplicate-reward and
protected-item invariant results, the manifest/catalogue comparison, and the
unresolved GATE-0004 record.

## Acceptance criteria

- Eligibility is validated before inventory or party-progression mutation.
- Duplicate rewards are ignored and required items remain protected from
  destructive progression states or dead ends.
- No authored values, UI, asset, audio, or economy feature is introduced; GATE-
  0004 remains explicit for concrete content implementation.
