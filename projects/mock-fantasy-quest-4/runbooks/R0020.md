## Objective

Document the SHARD-02 campaign-domain ownership index, unresolved gates,
asset/provenance handoff, and explicit boundary to SHARD-03. This is a
developer-documentation contract and does not implement UI or PWA behaviour.

## Dependencies

R0017 documents the progression integration, R0018 documents package assembly
and asset/provenance reservation, and R0019 documents invariant coverage. This
handoff records their interfaces and limits without assuming their completion
resolves GATE-0004 or human review.

## Ownership

- `docs/campaign-domain-boundary.md`.
- SHARD-02 ownership index, retained-gate register, and SHARD-03 handoff.
- Documented handoff for campaign-domain contracts and validated package input.

## Required behaviour

Record the owner and public boundary for scenes/exits, quest/tutorial state,
encounters/rewards, inventory/party progression, campaign completion, content
package/provenance, and campaign negative fixtures. State that GATE-0004 blocks
concrete authored campaign implementation and that human creative/provenance
review is mandatory before content admission; neither is an automated or
documentation approval. Identify SHARD-03 as the owner of session, UI, input,
accessibility, responsive presentation, and browser/PWA adaptation, while the
shared core supplies deterministic commands/state only.

## Web and PWA differences

Document the intentional boundary: responsive web and installed PWA consume
the same campaign rules and validated content, but their UI, input adapters,
cache/install/update lifecycle, and release delivery are not SHARD-02 work.

## Non-goals

Do not implement UI, PWA delivery, browser tests, rendering, input mappings,
asset production, audio, release claims, product changes, or gate approvals.

## Asset assessment

`asset_impact` is `not_applicable`: this documentation creates no visual or
source asset. Do not create or reserve any product asset; `asset_ids` remains
blank. R0018 remains the sole owner of its reserved campaign asset IDs.

## Audio assessment

`audio_impact` is `not_applicable`: no cue, playback behavior, or mix belongs
to this documentation handoff. Keep audio metadata blank/default and create no
audio asset.

- SHARD-03 receives a clear documented handoff for UI, input, accessibility,
  responsive web, and installed-PWA responsibilities.
