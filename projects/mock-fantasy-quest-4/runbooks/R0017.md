## Objective

Specify the shared-core integration that joins the abstract town-to-dungeon
route, required quest/tutorial progress, encounter rewards, and protected
inventory into one safely completable campaign ending. It owns integration
rules, not authored story or presentation.

## Dependencies

R0013 owns scene transitions, R0014 owns quest and tutorial transitions, R0015
owns encounter/reward resolution, and R0016 owns inventory/party safety. This
contract composes their public outcomes without transferring their ownership.
GATE-0004 remains an implementation gate for concrete campaign content.

## Ownership

- `src/core/campaign/progression*` and `tests/core/campaign/progression*`.
- Campaign route graph, integration checkpoints, completion-state envelope,
  and the campaign-completion command.
- Cross-domain ordering rules from validated route through ending completion.

## Required behaviour

Accept completion only when a validated route has reached the authorised final
state: legal scene transition, required quest progression, resolved required
encounter/reward outcome, and protected-item predicates must all hold. Validate
expected revision and completion eligibility before mutation. Completion emits
one stable result and is idempotent: a duplicate or stale completion request
returns the recorded result without replaying grants, transitions, or ending
effects. Invalid, blocked, or dead-end routes preserve state with typed
rejection; optional rewards cannot be completion prerequisites.

## Web and PWA differences

Web and installed-PWA adapters submit the same completion command and render
its state/event. Ending UI, navigation, animation, cache updates, persistence,
and installation lifecycle are outside this shared-core contract.

## Non-goals

Do not author town/dungeon maps, characters, dialogue, ending text, assets,
audio, UI, extra regions, branching stories, remote content, or save drivers.
Do not resolve GATE-0004.

## Asset assessment

`asset_impact` is `not_applicable`: this is a content-neutral integration
contract. Do not create or reserve maps, illustrations, sprites, textures,
portraits, icons, fonts, or other product assets; `asset_ids` remains blank.

## Audio assessment

`audio_impact` is `not_applicable`: the contract does not select, trigger, or
mix music or sound effects. Keep all audio fields blank/default and do not
create incidental cues.

## Verification

- Use deterministic abstract fixtures to prove exactly one start-to-ending
  route is reachable only after required cross-domain predicates hold.
- Prove blocked routes, stale commands, and duplicate completion requests
  preserve state or return the recorded completion result; no reward repeats.
- Compare metadata, requirements, ownership, provenance, and dependencies to
  IMP-0017 and its manifest allocation; retain GATE-0004 explicitly.

## Evidence

Retain reachability output, rejection and duplicate-completion fixtures, the
manifest/catalogue comparison, and the unresolved GATE-0004 record.

## Acceptance criteria

- One validated abstract route reaches campaign completion without a dead end.
- Ending completion is revision-checked and idempotent; duplicate effects and
  reward grants cannot occur.
- Concrete authored content, UI, assets, audio, and GATE-0004 approval remain
  outside this contract.
