## Objective

Define original-content-neutral dialogue, quest, tutorial, and campaign-state
rules that make required progression explicit and idempotent. This contract
defines progression mechanics, not story text, presentation, or authored
campaign expression.

## Dependencies

R0004 owns the core command/idempotency contract and R0013 owns scene and
interaction transitions. Their outputs are prerequisites, not transferred
ownership. GATE-0004 remains an implementation gate for authored text, locale,
and content detail; it does not defer this contract's authoring.

## Ownership

- `src/core/campaign/{quest,dialogue,tutorial}*` and
  `tests/core/campaign/quest*`.
- Quest, dialogue, tutorial, predicate, and progression envelopes.
- Shared progression commands and idempotent completion/rejection rules.

## Required behaviour

Represent progression as stable identifiers, explicit states, prerequisites,
and predicates. Validate interaction IDs, expected revisions, state
preconditions, and predicate inputs before completing tutorial or quest steps.
Completion is idempotent: repeating an acknowledged interaction returns its
prior outcome and never repeats a transition, reward, or required-state loss.
Invalid predicates and missing prerequisites reject safely without mutation.
Required campaign progression cannot be bypassed, overwritten, or made
unreachable by optional state.

## Web and PWA differences

Web and PWA adapters render and submit the same shared progression commands.
They may provide presentation-specific onboarding affordances but have no
authority to mark dialogue, tutorial, or quest progress complete. UI flow,
layout, localization rendering, and PWA lifecycle are excluded.

## Non-goals

Do not author story, dialogue, locale strings, characters, branches, user
content, UI, assets, audio, maps, rewards, browser flows, or remote state. Do
not resolve GATE-0004 or select concrete campaign content.

## Asset assessment

`asset_impact` is `not_applicable`: predicate and progression contracts need
no visual/source asset. Do not create or reserve dialogue portraits, maps,
icons, illustrations, fonts, sprites, or any other product asset; `asset_ids`
remains blank.

## Audio assessment

`audio_impact` is `not_applicable`: state transitions neither select nor
trigger music, speech, or sound effects. Keep all audio metadata blank/default
and do not create incidental cues.

## Verification

- Exercise repeated completion through deterministic fixtures and prove it
  returns the acknowledged outcome without an extra transition or reward.
- Prove invalid predicates, missing prerequisites, stale revisions, and unknown
  identifiers reject without state mutation or bypassing required progress.
- Compare metadata, requirements, provenance, dependencies, and ownership to
  IMP-0014 and R0014's manifest allocation; retain GATE-0004 explicitly.

## Evidence

Retain progression state-machine fixture output, idempotency/rejection results,
the manifest/catalogue comparison, and the unresolved GATE-0004 record.

## Acceptance criteria

- Quest and tutorial progression is explicit, predicate-checked, repeat-safe,
  and cannot bypass required campaign state.
- Repeated completion is idempotent and invalid progression commands preserve
  the prior state.
- No authored narrative, locale, UI, asset, or audio work is introduced, and
  GATE-0004 remains visible as an implementation gate.
