## Objective

Define the shared, content-neutral scene, exit, collision, and interaction
transition contract that preserves a completable route. This task owns rules
and envelopes only, not maps, presentation, or authored campaign content.

## Dependencies

R0004 provides immutable state, commands, events, and invariants; R0005
provides the content schema and reference validator. Both remain their owners.
GATE-0004 remains an implementation gate for concrete scene, locale, map, and
content shapes; it does not defer this contract's authoring.

## Ownership

- `src/core/campaign/scene*` and `tests/core/campaign/scene*`.
- The scene and exit envelope, including stable scene/exit/interaction IDs.
- Shared scene-transition commands and route-graph validation boundary.

## Required behaviour

Model scene entry, available exits, collision/interaction eligibility, and
transition outcomes as deterministic shared-core data. Validate references,
the expected revision, current-scene membership, predicate eligibility, and
destination reachability before mutating state. An invalid, blocked, stale, or
unknown exit returns a typed rejection and leaves state unchanged. A validated
route graph must identify a start-to-completion path without adding concrete
map geometry or authored locations.

## Web and PWA differences

Responsive web and installed PWA adapters submit the same scene commands and
render returned state/events. They may translate input or visual collision
signals into commands but cannot interpret or mutate campaign transitions.
Rendering, navigation layout, browser history, cache lifecycle, and PWA
installation are outside this shared-core contract.

## Non-goals

Do not create UI, maps, extra regions, procedural worlds, authored locations,
dialogue, assets, audio, input handling, browser navigation, persistence
drivers, or concrete content values. Do not resolve GATE-0004.

## Asset assessment

`asset_impact` is `not_applicable`: scene envelopes and validation do not need
visual or source assets. Do not create or reserve incidental maps, tiles,
sprites, icons, textures, illustrations, fonts, or other product assets;
`asset_ids` remains blank.

## Audio assessment

`audio_impact` is `not_applicable`: this contract neither selects nor triggers
music or sound effects. Keep audio IDs, brief, and style version blank; do not
create incidental cues.

## Verification

- Use deterministic fixtures to show invalid exits, unknown references, stale
  commands, and blocked predicates leave the exact prior state unchanged.
- Validate a minimal abstract route graph and reject an unreachable destination
  or invalid exit reference without introducing authored maps or locations.
- Compare metadata, ownership, requirements, provenance, and dependencies with
  IMP-0013 and the R0013 manifest allocation; retain GATE-0004 explicitly.

## Evidence

Retain fixture output for rejected and legal transitions, route-graph validator
results, the ownership comparison, and the recorded unresolved GATE-0004.

## Acceptance criteria

- Scene, exit, collision, and interaction transitions are shared-core,
  deterministic, reference-checked, and content-neutral.
- Invalid exits and references do not mutate state, and route validation proves
  a completable abstract path.
- GATE-0004 remains explicit for concrete content implementation; no UI,
  authored map, visual asset, or audio work is introduced.
