## Objective

Define the original-content provenance control that makes each later authored
content package auditable before it enters a build. The control supplies a
provenance-record format, source-asset manifest input, validator contract, and
human creative-review disposition without creating art, audio, story, or legal
approval.

## Dependencies

R0005 provides the versioned content package, asset/provenance-manifest schema,
and validator boundary. It is the implementation dependency. Human creative
review is mandatory for every authored package; legal or licensing review,
where applicable, remains under GATE-0007 and cannot be inferred or automated.

## Ownership

- `assets/source-manifest.*` provenance input format for later authored
  content/assets.
- `docs/original-content-review.md` review checklist and recorded disposition.
- `tests/content/provenance.*` missing-field and review-state fixtures.
- Provenance manifest, review disposition data, and provenance-validator input
  contract.

R0005 retains content-schema and registry ownership. This task owns only the
evidence required to admit later authored material; it does not own assets,
licences, gameplay content, or release packaging.

## Required behaviour

Require a stable content-package reference and a complete provenance record
for every later authored content, visual, audio, localisation, map, dialogue,
or other expressive entry. The record must identify the entry, its authoring
source/type, original-work assertion, any declared permitted source or licence
reference when applicable, review status, reviewer identity/role placeholder,
review date placeholder, and a human creative-review disposition. Keep the
functional reference boundary explicit: a compact party-based turn-adventure
scope may inform function only; names, lore, text, art, music, maps, layouts,
UI flows, interaction choreography, and other expression must be original.

The validator rejects a missing record, missing required fields, unmatched
content reference, invalid disposition, or an entry not marked ready after
human creative review. It must not infer originality from an asset filename,
automatically clear legal rights, or create substitute content. A reviewer may
record a hold or reject disposition; only the human reviewer can record the
creative-review outcome. Where a declared source signals licensing or legal
review, retain GATE-0007 rather than allowing a technical validator to approve
it.

## Web and PWA differences

The provenance control is shared content metadata and has no separate web or
PWA behaviour. Responsive web and installed PWA consume only content that has
passed the same provenance validation; installation, caching, and packaging do
not create a provenance exception.

## Non-goals

Do not create or select art, audio, dialogue, maps, story, UI, fonts, icons,
assets, licences, legal advice, automated legal clearance, release packaging,
or target-specific content. Do not treat a reference, filename, or automated
check as human creative or legal approval.

## Asset assessment

`asset_impact` is `not_applicable`. This is a control and review contract for
future asset-required work; it creates no product asset. Do not create or
reserve visual, source, audio, icon, illustration, font, texture, sprite, map,
or other incidental asset; `asset_ids` and audio IDs remain blank.

## Verification

- Run validator fixtures proving missing provenance, required-field omissions,
  unmatched IDs, invalid review dispositions, and unreviewed entries are
  rejected before content admission.
- Run a valid synthetic record fixture with an explicit original-work assertion
  and recorded human creative-review disposition; it must be traceable to its
  content-package reference.
- Review the checklist against the originality boundary and prove that it
  retains functional-only reference use and records a hold for any applicable
  GATE-0007 legal/licensing concern.
- Before recording the B0005 checkpoint, load this runbook and compare its
  metadata, provenance, dependencies, requirement IDs, and ownership to the
  manifest. Stop on a mismatch; retain R0011 and never renumber or replace it.

## Evidence

Retain rejected-fixture results, the valid synthetic record and reviewer
disposition, the originality-boundary checklist result, any GATE-0007 hold,
and the successful loader and manifest-comparison record.

## Acceptance criteria

- Missing or invalid provenance records prevent later authored content from
  entering a build; validated records remain traceable to their package entry.
- A human creative review disposition is recorded for each authored package;
  automated validation does not claim creative, legal, or licensing approval.
- The original-expression boundary and any applicable GATE-0007 review remain
  explicit, with no asset, audio, or copied expression introduced.
