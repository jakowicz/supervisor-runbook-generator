## Objective

Run one durable authoring-dispatch iteration after D0001. Expand at most one
eligible queued specification/catalogue chapter into no more than 250 `IMP-*`
records, validate it, then allocate ready unassigned contracts into at most ten
new B-series authoring tasks with no more than seven R-series outputs each.
Create exactly one successor dispatcher when queued work or ready contracts
remain; otherwise create exactly one final audit B-series task. Exit after that
bounded set.

## Context packet

Read `INITIAL.md`, `../PROJECT_BRIEF.md`, `../specification/README.md`,
`../planning/implementation-catalogue-index.json`,
`../planning/runbook-authoring-manifest.json`, its Markdown ledger, and
`../../../runbooks/F014.md`. Review D0001's `dispatch_history` checkpoint and
only the canonical specification sections needed for a selected chapter. Gates
block implementation, not catalogue expansion or R-contract authoring: retain
`DEC-0004`/`GATE-0004` and other implementation gates explicitly on the
resulting contracts without treating them as authoring blockers. `SHARD-05`
remains held unless explicit authorisation is recorded.

## Output list

- No R-series output is preassigned. Allocate R IDs only for ready, unassigned
  catalogue contracts discovered after the bounded chapter check.
- At most ten new B-series authoring runbooks, each owning no more than seven
  newly assigned R-series outputs.
- Exactly one successor dispatcher or one final audit B-series task, selected
  only after the queue and ready-contract check.

## Required behaviour

Preserve all accepted work and use monotonic B/R/IMP IDs. Do not infer approval
from a gate or from a queued state. A selected expansion must include stable
requirement links, ownership, dependencies, non-goals, platform distinctions,
verification, asset assessment, and canonical sources; validate coverage,
unique IDs, ownership, provenance, and acyclic dependencies before allocation.
Every new B task must provide its context packet, output list, validation,
checkpoint/update procedure, collision protection, and stop condition. Do not
create R-series files in this dispatcher.

## Validation procedure

Validate JSON, the selected chapter's 250-contract limit, unique IMP/B/R IDs,
dependency closure and acyclicity, ownership/path collisions, allocation
uniqueness, provenance, and asset metadata. Confirm each B owns at most seven R
IDs and that no more than ten B tasks were created. Confirm the successor/final
audit choice against the remaining queued chapters and ready unassigned
contracts. Stop and report any source conflict, missing coverage, cycle,
collision, unresolved authority, or need to overwrite accepted work.

## Checkpoint and update procedure

Atomically update the JSON and Markdown ledgers with chapter state, allocations,
counters, and the D0002 checkpoint only after validation. Retain reserved IDs
on repair; never renumber, replace, or rewrite an accepted B/R contract.

## Collision protection

Before creating any B, R, IMP, successor, or audit path, verify that its ID and
path are absent from the authoritative manifest and filesystem. On collision,
stop without overwriting or reusing the ID.

## Stop condition

Stop immediately after at most one chapter expansion, one allocation wave, and
exactly one successor dispatcher or final audit task. This is not a recursive
prompt and must not materialise a whole-game plan.

## Acceptance criteria

- The iteration expands at most one eligible chapter and adds no more than 250 IMP contracts.
- It creates at most ten bounded B-series authoring tasks, each with at most seven assigned R-series outputs.
- Every created B task has its context packet, output list, validation, checkpoint/update, collision protection, and stop condition.
- If queued or ready work remains, exactly one successor dispatcher is created; otherwise exactly one final audit B-series task is created.
- IDs, manifest ownership, provenance, asset assessments, and dependencies are validated without overwriting accepted work.
