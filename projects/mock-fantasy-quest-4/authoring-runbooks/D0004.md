## Objective

Run one bounded dispatch iteration after D0003: expand at most one eligible
chapter into at most 250 IMP records, allocate ready contracts into at most ten
B batches of at most seven R outputs, then create one successor or final audit.

## Context packet

Read the project brief, canonical specification, catalogue, JSON/Markdown
ledger, D0003 checkpoint, and F014. Keep every human gate explicit; SHARD-05
remains held without recorded authorisation.

## Output list

Allocate only newly ready unassigned contracts and create exactly one successor
dispatcher while queued/authorable work remains, otherwise one final audit B task.

## Validation procedure

Validate JSON, chapter limit, unique monotonic IDs, dependency closure and
acyclicity, ownership, provenance, allocation uniqueness, and asset/audio metadata.

## Checkpoint and update procedure

Update both ledgers only after validation. Retain IDs on repair and never
overwrite, renumber, or regenerate accepted B/R work.

## Collision protection

Verify every new ID/path is absent from the manifest and filesystem before use;
stop on collision, source conflict, or unresolved authority.

## Stop condition

Stop after one chapter expansion, one allocation wave, and one successor/audit;
do not recursively materialise later work.

## Acceptance criteria

- The iteration expands at most one eligible chapter and adds no more than 250 IMP contracts.
- It creates at most ten B-series authoring tasks, each with at most seven R-series outputs.
- Every created authoring task has objective, validation, checkpoint, stop, and acceptance criteria sections.
- Exactly one successor dispatcher or final audit task is created after queue and ready-work review.
- IDs, ownership, provenance, dependencies, and asset/audio metadata are validated without overwriting accepted work.
