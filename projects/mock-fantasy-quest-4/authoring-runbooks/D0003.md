## Objective

Run one durable dispatch iteration after D0002: expand at most one eligible
queued chapter into no more than 250 IMP records, allocate ready unassigned
contracts to at most ten B batches of at most seven R outputs, then create
exactly one successor dispatcher while queue/ready work remains, otherwise one
final audit B task.

authoritative JSON/Markdown manifest, D0002 checkpoint, and F014. Gates remain

## Output list

B tasks and exactly one successor dispatcher or final audit B task.

## Validation procedure

Validate JSON, the selected chapter limit, monotonic unique IMP/B/R IDs,
dependency closure/acyclicity, output ownership, provenance, allocation
uniqueness, asset metadata, and B limits. Verify the successor/final choice
against remaining queue and ready contracts.

## Checkpoint and update procedure

Update JSON and Markdown ledgers only after validation. Retain IDs on repair;
never overwrite, renumber, or regenerate accepted B/R work.

## Collision protection

Before creating an ID or path, verify it is absent from the manifest and
filesystem. Stop on a collision or unresolved authority.

## Stop condition

Stop after one chapter expansion, one allocation wave, and exactly one
successor or audit task; do not recursively materialise later work.

## Acceptance criteria

- The iteration expands at most one eligible chapter and adds no more than 250
  `IMP-*` records.
- It creates at most ten B-series authoring tasks, each assigned no more than
  seven R-series contracts.
- Every new B, C, D, or final-audit authoring runbook parses with the
  Supervisor loader and includes objective, validation, checkpoint, stop, and
  acceptance-criteria sections before registration.
- It creates exactly one valid successor dispatcher while queued or authorable
  work remains; otherwise it creates exactly one final audit task.
- Manifest provenance, ownership, dependencies, asset/audio metadata, and IDs
  are validated without overwriting accepted work.
