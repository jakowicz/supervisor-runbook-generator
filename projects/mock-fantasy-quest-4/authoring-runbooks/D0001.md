## Objective

Run one durable authoring-dispatch iteration after the bootstrap B0001–B0006
wave. First expand at most one eligible queued specification/catalogue chapter
into no more than 250 `IMP-*` records and validate its requirement/dependency
coverage. Then allocate ready, unassigned contracts into at most ten new
B-series authoring tasks, each with at most seven R-series outputs. Create
exactly one successor dispatcher when a queued chapter or ready contract
remains; create the final audit B-series task instead only when neither exists.
Exit after creating that bounded set.

## Context packet

Read `INITIAL.md`, `../PROJECT_BRIEF.md`, `../specification/README.md`,
`../planning/implementation-catalogue-index.json`,
`../planning/runbook-authoring-manifest.json`, its Markdown ledger, and
`../../../runbooks/F014.md`. Read only the canonical specification sections
needed for the one selected chapter. Treat SHARD-05 as held unless explicit
authorisation is recorded; do not turn a gate into approval.

## Required behaviour

Select no more than one queued chapter whose dependencies and gates permit
planning. Add at most 250 stable `IMP-*` contracts with requirement links,
owned files/data/interfaces, dependencies, non-goals, platform distinctions,
verification, asset assessment, and canonical source links. Validate coverage,
unique IDs, ownership, and acyclic dependencies. Allocate only ready
unassigned contracts, using monotonic B/R counters and batches of no more than
seven R IDs; materialise at most ten corresponding B runbooks. Each B writer
must contain a context packet, output list, validation procedure,
checkpoint/update procedure, collision stop rule, and stop condition. Do not
create R-series files yourself.

## Checkpoint and update procedure

Atomically update the JSON/Markdown ledger counters, chapter state, contract
allocations, and checkpoint after validation. Never renumber, replace, or
rewrite an accepted B/R contract. If work remains, create exactly one new
dispatcher with the next monotonic dispatcher ID and a later sequence. If no
queued chapter or ready contract remains, create exactly one final audit
B-series runbook that validates all completed catalogue expansions and batches;
it must not pretend to generate or review all work in this dispatch.

## Validation procedure

Validate JSON, the 250-contract chapter cap, no more than ten newly-created B
files, no more than seven assigned R IDs per B file, unique B/R/IMP IDs,
dependency closure, provenance values, asset metadata, output ownership, and
the successor/final-audit choice. Stop and report a source conflict, missing
coverage, cycle, ID/path collision, unresolved authority, or any need to
overwrite accepted work.

## Stop condition

Stop immediately after one chapter expansion (or none), one allocation wave,
and exactly one successor dispatcher or final audit task. This is not a
recursive prompt and must not materialise a whole-game plan.

## Acceptance criteria

- This iteration expands at most one eligible chapter and adds no more than 250 IMP contracts.
- It creates at most ten bounded B-series authoring tasks, each with at most seven assigned R-series outputs.
- Every created B task has its context packet, output list, validation, checkpoint/update, collision protection, and stop condition.
- If queued or ready work remains, exactly one successor dispatcher is created; otherwise exactly one final audit B-series task is created.
- IDs, manifest ownership, provenance, asset assessments, and dependencies are validated without overwriting accepted work.
