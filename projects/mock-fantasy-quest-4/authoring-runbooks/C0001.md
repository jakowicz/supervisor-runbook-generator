## Objective

Validate the existing `SHARD-01` implementation catalogue before authoring.
Read only the canonical specification, `planning/implementation-catalogue-index.json`,
and `planning/runbook-authoring-manifest.json`. Confirm that SHARD-01 contains
at most 250 `IMP-*` contracts, that every ready foundation contract is allocated
once, and that SHARD-02 through SHARD-05 remain queued or held. Do not expand a
future chapter, create R-series files, or change accepted IDs.

## Validation procedure

Check unique `IMP-*`, B, and R IDs; contract-to-manifest coverage; dependency
closure; the 250-contract bound; and that B0001 through B0006 each contain no
more than seven R IDs. Record only factual repairs to the manifest/ledger when
the sources agree. Stop and report any missing canonical source, conflicting
ownership, cyclic dependency, or attempt to allocate a held connected-scope
chapter.

## Checkpoint and update procedure

Set the manifest wave checkpoint only after the structural checks pass. Preserve
all counters and existing allocations; append a short checkpoint note to the
ledger if it does not already describe the result. Leave future allocation to
D0001.

## Acceptance criteria

- SHARD-01 is verified as a bounded catalogue of no more than 250 contracts.
- The twelve foundation `IMP-*` records are each allocated exactly once to B0001 through B0006, with no batch over seven R contracts.
- Queued and held chapters remain unexpanded and no R-series implementation runbook is created.
- Conflicts, missing sources, cycles, or ID collisions stop the task without renumbering or overwriting work.
