[specification](../specification/README.md) and the allocation ledger at
[`../planning/runbook-authoring-manifest.json`](../planning/runbook-authoring-manifest.json)

output is registered as its own child collection.

foundation contracts. Future work is created only by the dispatcher, one
catalogue chapter and at most ten B-series tasks at a time.
