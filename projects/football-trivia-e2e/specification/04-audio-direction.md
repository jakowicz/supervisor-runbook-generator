# 04 — Audio direction

Audio is original, optional, captioned/described where it conveys state, and
always paired with a visual/text equivalent. No chant, anthem, broadcast,
club, or game imitation is permitted.

Final durations, quantities, loudness targets, loop policy, and generated-cue
selection need production approval; no audio is generated by this plan.

## Original-audio contract and cue IDs

Audio reinforces the original *matchday knowledge* arc: invitation, confident
answer, rising streak, decisive result, and clear return. It shares the abstract
broadcast-studio vocabulary of `01-creative-direction.md` and the journey map
in `04-experience-contract.md`: bright electronic percussion, soft claps, warm
brass-like synth accents, and abstract crowd-energy texture. It is not a real
crowd, chant, anthem, commentary, broadcaster ident, club sound, football-game
imitation, or vocal performance associated with a real team.

Cue IDs use `AUDIO-<FAMILY>-<NNN>` and are immutable after package reference.
Rendered variants append `-<state>-<mix>`; a manifest records ID, source-session
or render checksum, creator/provenance, licence, loop points, loudness/peak,
duration, caption/description key, trigger, mix group, and review state.
Essential state also has text, icon/pattern, and semantic announcement. Captions
describe function (for example, “brief rising streak cue”); missing audio never
blocks an action.

P0 is instrumental only: no intelligible vocals, sampled speech, commentary,
chants, crowd recordings, or copyrighted musical imitation. Original/properly
licensed sources only; generated cues retain settings and receive human
originality review.

## Playback and mixing policy

Provide persistent per-local-profile master mute and independent music/feedback
controls. Honour platform/system volume, audio focus/interruption, and exposed
reduced-sensory preferences. Backgrounding pauses nonessential playback and
never affects game state. Do not autoplay audible music before player
interaction where platform policy requires it.

The same IDs serve responsive web, PWA, Android, and iPhone. Web/PWA waits for
audio unlock and silently retains captions/text if unavailable; phones respect
mute switch/audio focus. Keyboard, touch, mouse, and gamepad invoke the same
semantic state and therefore the same cue. Remote/TV delivery is out of P0.

Production tests cover speakers/headphones, system muted, audio unavailable,
screen-reader speech, interruption, offline cached assets, and reduced feedback.
Acceptance requires intended trigger once at safe state, correct loop/duration,
no required audio-only meaning, caption key, mute/volume support, permitted
source, and complete manifest provenance/checksum/review. Exact final rendered
count, loudness readings, formats, size budget, and language captions await
production approval; this plan creates or licenses no audio.
