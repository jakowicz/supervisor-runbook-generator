# 01 — Creative direction

**Inputs:** [`INITIAL.md`](../INITIAL.md) and
[`PROJECT_BRIEF.md`](../PROJECT_BRIEF.md). This is the early creative north star
for content, feature, art, audio, and implementation work.

## Narrative/gameplay framing and voice

The player is a quick-thinking football devotee on a short “matchday knowledge”
challenge: recognise a clue, make a confident call, and build momentum through a
themed round. The play space is an original abstract broadcast studio, not a
named stadium, club, league, publisher, or existing product. The goal is to
finish a curated round with a strong score/streak and learn from clear feedback.

The engagement arc is invitation, first confidence, rising streak tension, a
decisive final prompt, then a clear celebration or learning-oriented result.
First-release content needs questions, themes, answer explanations/feedback,
local milestones, and results/completion states. It needs no fictional
characters, lore, RPG party, quests, bosses, combat, or narrative ending. The
brief's campaign/battle contradiction requires approval before such content is
produced.

Writing is precise, lively, inclusive, and fact-first: short prompts,
unambiguous instructions, plain-language adjudication, and celebratory but
non-taunting feedback. Authors avoid stereotypes, unreviewed claims, and
imitation of reference publications.

## Visual and audio intent

Visual direction: original modern football-broadcast graphics, bold accessible
category colours, large score numerals, readable typography, disciplined card
geometry, clear answer states, and restrained motion. Prompt hierarchy comes
first, answer action second, score/streak third. Colour is never the only cue;
the system supports adjustable text, focus, reduced motion, high contrast,
captions, and responsive portrait-first layouts. Use original abstract pitch
lines, stat blocks, signal sweeps, and celebration shapes—never real club,
competition, broadcaster, publisher, or QuizUp marks, kit designs, screenshots,
or screen composition.

Audio direction: an original restrained percussive entry bed; distinct correct,
incorrect, streak, optional urgency, and completion cues; and a warm short
results sting. Bright electronic percussion, claps, soft brass-like synth
accents, and abstract crowd energy are allowed; sampled or imitated chants,
anthems, broadcasts, clubs, or games are not. Essential meaning always has
text/visual alternatives plus volume, mute, and caption/description options.
Exact cue quantities/durations/loop policy remain production decisions.

The Athletic and QuizUp are functional references only: no copying of branding,
questions, wording, copy, art, audio, layouts, flow choreography, or distinctive
interactions. All expression is independently authored.
